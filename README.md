# imok 1v1

A blocky three.js shooter you play in the browser against bots: 1v1, 2v2 or free for all.

**Duels** – pick **1v1**, **2v2** (you and a bot teammate against two bots; teammates spawn side by side, can't hurt each other, and once you're out you spectate your teammate) or **Free for all** (you against three bots, everyone for themselves). 1v1 and 2v2 are first to five rounds; Free for all is a deathmatch with no rounds: everyone respawns 3 seconds after going out, at the spawn point furthest from the others, and the first to 10 kills wins (10-minute limit, most kills wins if time runs out). Maps: Maple Street and Wreck Cove (Sky Islands and Rail Yard are coming soon and show as locked tiles in the menu). On Maple Street every house can be entered; most have two floors (each staircase ends on a landing with a wide, open side onto the floor, so you step straight off at the top), balconies, lofts with rooftop terraces, and big upstairs windows you can walk out of. Ledges and air-con units under the side windows let you climb onto the roofs, and every pitched roof can be walked on. The two big end houses also have a hop-up (a bin and two crates) against one side that takes you up onto the window ledge and in through an upstairs window. Rooms are tall (about 4 m downstairs, 3.6 m upstairs and in the lofts), so the houses don't feel cramped. Each house has its own roof colour so you can call out positions. Parked cars (with spinning hubcaps that blur at speed and kick up a little dust) pull out now and then and drive along the street at their own speed (they stop for players and walls), birds fly over, clouds drift, trees sway and butterflies flutter round the front gardens. **Wreck Cove** is a sunny cove closed in by cliffs and headlands. A galleon lies broken in two on the beach: both halves have a lower gun deck, a main deck and stairs, the stern has the captain's cabin under a high castle, the bow a raised forecastle, and the fallen mainmast bridges the two. Around it: a thatched Tiki Bar (crates up onto the roof), a lifeguard tower, a row of beach huts, a rock lookout up the cliff, a volleyball court, dunes, rocks, umbrellas and a bonfire. A jetty runs out to a moored fishing boat, and there's a swim raft, a swan pedalo and rocks in the shallows. The sea is the star: a swell rolls in every few seconds, rises over the shallows and breaks into a white curl and a foaming bore with spray, then rushes up the sand as a thin sheet edged with bubbly foam and slides back leaving lace and dark wet sand. Foam swirls in the shallows, sunlight ripples over the sand under the water, and the surface glitters in the sun (phones get a lighter version). You can wade out to the buoy line, a little slower: every step splashes, landing in the water makes a bigger splash, and bullets, thrown items and explosions all splash where they hit the water. Sailing boats, a yacht, a speedboat and a cargo ship pass offshore, gulls circle and crabs scuttle along the waterline. Each round you and the bot start at spawn points far apart: out in the street, up on roofs and upper floors, or inside houses. The bot climbs stairs and roofs too.

**Bot play styles** – every round each bot secretly picks one of ten styles (and weapons to match), so you have to work out what you're up against:
- **Rusher** – charges straight at you, jukes and jumps, never retreats.
- **Sniper** – takes high ground far away, scopes in, relocates after every couple of shots, switches to its sidearm if you get close.
- **Lurker** – hides out of sight near you and springs when you come close or turn your back.
- **Flanker** – circles round out of view and hits you from the side.
- **Turtle** – walls up when it sees you, peeks from cover, backs off when hurt.
- **Duelist** – strafes and trades shots at mid range.
- **Hunter** – always has a rough fix on where you are and keeps tracking you down.
- **Breacher** – shotgun; hops between hiding spots, each one closer, then bursts in.
- **Kiter** – holds its favourite range: backs off as you push, follows as you run.
- **Camper** – picks one spot with a view, walls up and holds the angle all round.

All styles push when you're low, play safer when hurt, and chase the clock late in a round when behind on health. Sniper, Turtle, Kiter and Camper aren't used in Rumble.

**Rumble** – Duels with no guns (also 1v1, 2v2 or free for all): fists only (18 per punch), on the small Backyard map or The House. Punch someone while you're falling for 20 damage: you both get knocked back and they're stunned for a second. Abilities, walls, pick-ups, slams and finishing moves all still work.

The menu is a full-screen, three-step setup: 1) mode and map, 2) character (with a profile preview; Blaze is selected by default) and consumable, 3) primary and secondary weapons (with previews and stats). Start turns green once everything is picked.

The menu opens with everything pre-picked so you can hit Start straight away: the top map, Blaze (top left of the character grid), HDR, MP5 and Grenade.

**Shop** – every map has an open SHOP kiosk on the clearest ground near the middle, reachable from all sides; the edges of its roof glow so you can spot it. Walk up to it and a quick menu pops up: press 1–4 (or tap) to take one item – **Health** (+50 HP), **Armour** (blocks the next 50 damage), **Scope** (the red-dot sight on the AKM, M4, LMG, MP5 and Uzi, with steadier aim; kept while you keep winning rounds) or **Grenades** (3 more; if your consumable is the grenade they simply add to your G count, otherwise they're thrown with T). In Rumble it's Health, Armour or **Speed** (+25% for 30 s). After a pick the shop restocks for you in 30 seconds. The fight doesn't pause while you're there. Unused shop items carry into the next round only if you (or your team) won the round; lose or draw and they're gone.

Characters (12): Brute (a slate-skinned giant with rage veins; 130 HP, leaps and smashes the ground for up to 48 damage and knockback, breaking nearby walls), Jet (an ace pilot with a chrome jetpack; flight), Blaze (a fire brawler with flame hair; fireballs), Blink (a street magician; teleport dash), Tank (a mech pilot in a yellow exo-frame; 150 HP and a shield), Ghost (a hooded spectre; invisibility), Frost (an ice mage; frost nova that slows anyone within 9 m), Patch (a field medic; heals 48 HP), Volt (an electric punk with tesla coils; 36-damage lightning bolt), Hook (a climber; grapples to wherever you aim), Zip (a sprinter; fastest runner, 90 HP, sprint burst) and Thorn (a bark-armoured warden; blocks 30% and reflects 60% of the damage taken), each with an ability on E.

**Stamina and regen** – hold Shift to run (about 40% faster; on touch, push the move stick all the way out). The stamina bar under your health lasts about 5 seconds of running, refills a second after you stop, and once it's empty you can't run again until it's a quarter full. Getting shot drains stamina too, more for harder hits (an HDR body shot takes most of it, an SMG bullet a sliver). Health regenerates once you've gone 5 seconds without being hit: 6 HP a second up to half health, then 3 HP a second; bots regenerate too.

**HUD** – everyone moves 25% faster than before. Bottom left is a vitals panel with health (green), armour (blue) and stamina (purple) bars in that order, and above it a compact items row: an icon, its key and how many you have (ability, consumable, walls, plus shop grenades and speed boots when you have them). Top left is a small leaderboard of everyone in the match with kills, assists (damaged someone a teammate or rival then eliminated within 15 s) and deaths; anyone out for the current round is greyed. Top right is a minimap that turns with you and shows the map layout, the shop (gold square) and teammates (blue). Enemies never show unless they fire: each shot leaves a tiny red dot where they were, fading over a couple of seconds. Whenever anyone is eliminated a small red X marks the spot for 3 seconds. While you're out, the vitals and weapon panels switch to the player you're watching (name, kills/assists/deaths, live health, armour, stamina, gun and ammo); in deathmatch you watch whoever got you until you respawn. Being stunned puts a black-and-yellow zigzag round the edge of the screen, being frozen by Frost a blue-and-white one, and taking a grenade or rocket blast a black-and-red one (no text).

**Pause menu** – Resume, Controls (every key, grouped), HUD layout and Home, plus touch controls and the two sensitivity sliders. HUD layout lets you switch any panel (score, leaderboard, minimap, kill feed, vitals, items, weapon and ammo, controls hint, name tags) on or off and make each one bigger or smaller (60–160%). Move panels lets you drag every panel to wherever you want it on screen (positions scale with the window); your layout is remembered, and Reset puts it back. Slow motion only kicks in for moments you're part of (your own down, kill or finishing move), never for other players' finishers. XP is only shown on the menu and the end-of-match screen.

**Replays and Home** – every kill you're part of (as killer, victim or with an assist), finishing moves included, is kept as a short replay of what you saw. At the end of each round a bar shows a replay button per kill plus Home (press 1–9 to watch one, H to go home; the mouse stays captured so you can keep looking around); the end-of-match screen lists every replay from the match next to Rematch and Home. Watching a replay pauses the game; Save video downloads it as a video file. Home is also on the pause menu.

**Damage** – every weapon and ability hits 20% harder than it used to (Blaze's fireball 54, Volt's bolt 36, Brute's smash up to 48, Frost nova 12; Patch heals 48 and Thorn reflects 60%), so fights end a little quicker.

**Reviving** – stand over a downed teammate and they glow green and revive twice as fast (2 seconds instead of 4).

**End of match** – the result, then "You caught the W!" or "Took the L this time :(", the score and four stats: Kills, Damage, Accuracy, Headshots. Rematch, View Clips (every replay from the match) and Home.

**Lethals** (what used to be called consumables; press G): Grenade, Stim, Stun Grenade and Sense Pack as before, plus:
- **Flash Bang** (2 per round) – thrown like a grenade; anyone who can see the blast gets a near-white screen for 2 seconds (1 second if facing away). Bots lose track of you for as long.
- **Proximity Mine** (2 per round) – laid on the floor in front of you, armed after a second (fast-blinking red light while arming), goes off when an enemy comes within about 1.7 m (60 damage); it's dark grey with a dim red blink, so it's easy to miss. Other blasts set it off too.
- **Cluster Grenade** (2 per round) – a small burst that throws out five bomblets, each a smaller, weaker blast (24 damage) a moment later.
- **Golden Armour** (always on) – you start every round or respawn with 50 armour, which rebuilds back to 50 at a quarter of the speed health regenerates.

**Deaths and celebrations** – an ordinary kill drops the body flat on the ground with a few small red blocks round it (no explosion, no slow motion); only finishing moves blow people apart. Downing someone no longer slows the game either. If you finish a downed enemy while every one of their teammates is also down, the whole team is out. When a round or match is won, every winner still standing flexes, spins, jumps or blasts their gun at the sky under sweeping neon beams, rainbow rings and fireworks; on a match win the camera swings round the winners. The kill feed sits under the score at 65% opacity.

**Cover** – bullets go through wood (fences, sheds, furniture, wooden floors and crates; each layer takes off 30% of the damage, up to three layers) but not through concrete, brick, walls, cars or built walls. Bodies can't pass through solid structures: throws and slams stop at walls, a Home run finisher stops at the first wall it hits, and anyone pushed into a wall is moved back out.

At 0 HP you go down instead of dying, with 30 downed health that takes full damage, so a few shots finish you: you crawl slowly, and if nobody finishes you, you revive automatically after 4 seconds at 10% health. While down, clicking fire tags the enemy you're aiming at so they're outlined in red for 3 seconds. In 2v2, if you're down and your teammate is eliminated, you're out too. The opponent can walk up and press F for their character's signature finishing move: Brute Pancake, Frost Ice sculpture, Jet Sky drop, Blaze Roast, Blink Sawn in half, Tank Knockout, Ghost Heartbreaker, Patch Clear!, Volt Overcharge, Hook Hot potato, Zip Blur rush, Thorn Dog pile (Gone fishing, Piledriver, Shadow grab and Overgrown are built but saved for later). If the finisher fires or gets shot mid-move the finisher is cancelled and the victim stays down. Every elimination plays the winner's kill effect: each character has two kill effects of their own. XP: +10 per round won, +5 per round lost, 100 XP per level. Built walls break apart brick by brick.

Each weapon's crosshair matches its colour. The HDR and Kar98 aim through a scope (the HDR's view is a little tighter), each with a red centre dot and a thin outline in the gun's colour. The shop's Scope adds a see-through red-dot ring sight to the AKM, M4, LMG, MP5 and Uzi. Weapons: pick one primary – HDR sniper (long range, a headshot downs in one, 2 body shots), Kar98 (short-range sniper), AKM, M4 (gold; harder hits, slower fire) or LMG – and one secondary – MP5, Uzi (heavier, steadier SMG), Rocket Launcher (slow-firing splash), Ninja Stars or Sword.

Lethals (press G; for grenades, hold G to see a clean dotted throw arc that stops at the first thing it would hit, with a circle there (the grenade itself still bounces and rolls as before), release to throw toward your crosshair; aiming with Ninja Stars shows their flight path too): Grenade (2 per round, 60 splash damage; thrown a long way, it explodes on anyone it hits, otherwise 2 seconds after it first lands, in a fireball with smoke and a scorch mark), Stim (+45% speed for 5s, 20s cooldown), Stun Grenade (2 per round, stuns anyone caught in the flash) or Sense Pack (see the enemy through walls for 6s, 25s cooldown). The bot gets a random one and uses it too. You also get four buildable walls per round. Magazines are small; reloads are quick, an empty gun in your hands starts reloading by itself after a short beat (even if you switched weapons mid-reload), and R reloads early.

Controls: WASD move · Shift run · Space jump · mouse aim (click to lock) · left click fire · right click aim down sights / scope · C crouch (toggle; running stands you up) / pick up a downed opponent · E ability · G consumable · T shop grenades (when your consumable isn't the grenade) · F finish · R reload · 1/2 or wheel switch · Q build wall · P or Esc pause (controls and sensitivity sliders are on the pause menu). Touch controls (move stick, drag to look, on-screen buttons) switch on automatically on phones and tablets and can be toggled on the pause menu.

**Parkour** – 12 obstacle courses with double jump, swings and moving platforms.

Static scenery is merged into a handful of meshes at load (instead of ~1,000 separate boxes, each with its own outline), which keeps the frame rate up on Maple Street.

It's a single static `index.html` (three.js and fonts load from CDNs), served by GitHub Pages. To play locally, open `index.html` in a browser.
